Candor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Candor is an iOS app that helps you communicate better at work. It suggests how to phrase a message, helps you prepare for hard conversations, and gives you a short daily practice plan, using AI-assisted suggestions. Candor is anonymous-first: you can use it without creating an account or giving us your name or email. Candor is operated by Manmeet Sethi as an individual developer. For any privacy question or data request, email m20sethi@gmail.com.

1. Data We Collect

2. How We Use Data

  • Provide the core features: Coach suggestions, vocabulary and interview lessons, and your daily practice plan.
  • Personalize what you practice based on the self-assessment and your progress.
  • Activate and manage your subscription.
  • Monitor reliability, debug errors, and improve product quality.
  • Measure advertising performance and app conversion events.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. AI Processing of What You Share

When you ask Coach for help, the text, attached screenshots, or voice you share are sent to a third-party AI model provider so it can generate suggestions for you. Please do not share other people's personal, confidential, or sensitive information that you are not permitted to share. We aim to minimize storing the raw workplace text you paste, and we do not use that raw text as analytics event content.

5. Advertising and Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)

On first launch Candor asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. The iOS Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is only collected if you allow it. With your permission, Candor uses Meta app events and the IDFA to measure app installs, registrations, and purchases. If you decline, the app still works; only the ad measurement is limited. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings, under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.

6. Payment Data

Payments are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. Candor does not receive or store your card or bank details. We store only the limited subscription and purchase identifiers needed to activate access, reconcile payments, and provide support.

7. Data Retention

We keep your profile, practice, usage, and subscription data while you use the app or as needed to provide the service. We may keep limited records for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and security purposes.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit and access controls, and we store data with reputable cloud providers. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Candor is anonymous-first: you can use it without giving us your name or email.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing m20sethi@gmail.com. Candor also supports deleting your profile and data from within the app where available.
  • You can decline or revoke ad tracking in iOS Settings, under Privacy and Security, then Tracking.
  • You can revoke microphone access anytime in iOS Settings for Candor.

10. Children's Privacy

Candor is intended for working professionals and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
